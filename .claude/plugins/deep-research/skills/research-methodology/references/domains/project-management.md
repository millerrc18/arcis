# Domain Preset: Project Management

## Description

Earned Value Management (EVM), critical path scheduling, risk management, program execution, integrated baseline reviews, and defense acquisition program management. Covers both traditional (waterfall/EVM) and modern (agile/hybrid) methodologies, with emphasis on DoD program management contexts.

## Preferred Sources

1. **DAU (Defense Acquisition University)** — ACQuipedia, Adaptive Acquisition Framework, guidebooks, continuous learning modules
2. **PMBOK (Project Management Body of Knowledge)** — PMI's standard reference
3. **EIA-748 (EVMS Standard)** — Earned Value Management Systems guidelines, 32 criteria
4. **NDIA EVMS guides** — Intent guides, surveillance guides, industry best practices
5. **Defense Acquisition University publications** — Defense AT&L magazine, acquisition research
6. **PMI publications** — PM Journal, PM Network, practice guides
7. **GAO Cost Estimating and Assessment Guide** — Government cost estimation best practices
8. **DoD Instruction 5000 series** — Acquisition policy directives
9. **IDA (Institute for Defense Analyses) papers** — Defense program analysis
10. **AACE International** — Cost engineering, total cost management, recommended practices

## Lateral Search Strategy

| Adjacent Field | Why Cross-Pollinate |
|---------------|-------------------|
| **Construction management** | Schedule compression techniques (crashing, fast-tracking), earned value origins, multi-trade coordination parallels multi-contractor programs |
| **Film production** | Complex project coordination with hard deadlines, creative/technical integration, union labor management, budget variance handling |
| **Military operations planning** | Mission planning frameworks (MDMP, JOPPA), contingency planning, wargaming as risk analysis |
| **Agile software development** | Sprint planning, velocity metrics, retrospectives — increasingly integrated with traditional EVM on DoD programs |
| **Systems engineering** | Requirements decomposition, technical performance measures, integration planning, V-model alignment with program milestones |

## Temporal Emphasis

Mixed. EVM fundamentals and scheduling theory are stable. Agile/hybrid methods and acquisition reform evolve continuously.

- **Half-life**: 3 years
- **Foundational corpus** (always relevant):
  - EIA-748 (EVMS Standard)
  - Fleming & Koppelman, *Earned Value Project Management*
  - Christensen, EVM research papers
  - Goldratt, *Critical Chain*
  - Hillson, *Managing Risk in Projects*
  - CPM/PERT foundational literature
- **Current emphasis**: DoD acquisition pathway updates (Adaptive Acquisition Framework), EVMS reform initiatives, agile-EVM integration methods, digital engineering implications for program management, should-cost analysis evolution.

## Output Template Tweaks

Add the following sections to the standard report template:

### EVM Metrics Applicability

[Identify which EVM metrics (CPI, SPI, TCPI, EAC, VAC, CV, SV) are most relevant to the topic. Discuss interpretation nuances, threshold values, and when standard EVM metrics may be misleading (e.g., early program, rebaseline, LOE-heavy WBS).]

### Risk Register Inputs

[Translate findings into risk register entries: risk statement (If-Then format), probability, consequence, risk exposure, mitigation strategy, and risk owner. Map to appropriate risk categories.]

### Schedule Impact Assessment

[Assess the impact on program schedule. Identify affected milestones, critical path implications, schedule margin consumption, and recommended schedule recovery options. Reference IMS best practices.]
