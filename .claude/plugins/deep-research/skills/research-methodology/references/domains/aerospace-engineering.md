# Domain Preset: Aerospace Engineering

## Description

Materials science, structural analysis, propulsion systems, avionics, flight systems, certification processes, and manufacturing methods as applied to aerospace platforms. Covers both commercial and defense aviation, with emphasis on airworthiness, qualification testing, and compliance with aerospace specifications and standards.

## Preferred Sources

1. **SAE International papers** — AMS specs, ARP standards, aerospace materials and processes
2. **NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS)** — Foundational aerospace research, freely available
3. **MIL-HDBK series** — Military handbooks (MIL-HDBK-5/MMPDS for metals, MIL-HDBK-17/CMH-17 for composites, MIL-HDBK-1823 for NDT)
4. **AIAA journals** — Journal of Aircraft, Journal of Propulsion and Power, AIAA Journal
5. **ASME standards and publications** — Pressure vessels, mechanical engineering fundamentals
6. **FAA Advisory Circulars (ACs)** — Certification guidance, approved methods
7. **FAA TSOs** — Technical Standard Orders for avionics and equipment
8. **Gulfstream specifications** — 3700ED drawing specs, GAMPS (General Aerospace Material & Process Specs), GAF14A codes
9. **ASTM standards** — Testing methods for materials and processes
10. **CMH-17 (Composite Materials Handbook)** — Composite design allowables and methods

## Temporal Emphasis

Foundational-heavy. Many governing standards and handbooks originate from the 1960s-1980s and remain authoritative. New research builds on rather than replaces this corpus.

- **Half-life**: 5 years
- **Foundational corpus** (always relevant):
  - MIL-HDBK-5 / MMPDS (metallic material allowables)
  - Bruhn, *Analysis and Design of Flight Vehicle Structures*
  - Niu, *Airframe Structural Design*
  - Peterson, *Stress Concentration Factors*
  - Bannantine, *Fundamentals of Metal Fatigue Analysis*
  - MIL-STD-1530 (Aircraft Structural Integrity Program)
- **Current emphasis**: New materials (additive manufacturing, advanced composites), updated certification requirements (AC 25.571, damage tolerance amendments), evolving NDT methods (phased array UT, digital radiography).
