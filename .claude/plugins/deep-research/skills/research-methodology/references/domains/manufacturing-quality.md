# Domain Preset: Manufacturing & Quality

## Description

Manufacturing processes, non-destructive testing (NDT/NDE), metrology and dimensional inspection, statistical process control (SPC), lean manufacturing, six sigma, first article inspection (FAI), process validation, and quality systems. Emphasis on aerospace manufacturing where process control and traceability are paramount.

## Preferred Sources

1. **ASM Handbooks** — Comprehensive reference for materials, heat treatment, surface engineering, failure analysis
2. **ASME standards** — Dimensional tolerancing (Y14.5), pressure vessels, mechanical testing
3. **ASNT (American Society for Nondestructive Testing)** — NDT methods, personnel qualification (SNT-TC-1A), technique development
4. **AMS (Aerospace Material Specifications)** — Material and process specs for aerospace
5. **SAE AS9102** — First Article Inspection standard and guidance
6. **Montgomery, *Statistical Quality Control*** — SPC textbook, foundational reference
7. **Quality Engineering** (journal) — Applied quality methods research
8. **NADCAP checklists and criteria** — Special process accreditation requirements
9. **NIST Measurement Services** — Calibration, measurement uncertainty, traceability
10. **Machining handbooks** — Machinery's Handbook, SME manufacturing references

## Temporal Emphasis

Foundational-heavy. The core principles of quality (Deming, Juran, Shewhart) and fundamental manufacturing processes are well-established and endure. New developments build on this stable foundation.

- **Half-life**: 5 years
- **Foundational corpus** (always relevant):
  - Shewhart, *Economic Control of Quality of Manufactured Product* (1931)
  - Deming, *Out of the Crisis*
  - Juran, *Quality Control Handbook*
  - Montgomery, *Introduction to Statistical Quality Control*
  - Taguchi methods (robust design)
  - Wheeler, *Understanding Variation*
- **Current emphasis**: Additive manufacturing quality, digital twin for process control, automated inspection (machine vision, CT scanning), Industry 4.0 sensor integration, updated NADCAP criteria.
