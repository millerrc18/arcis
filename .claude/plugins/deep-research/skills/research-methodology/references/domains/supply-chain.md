# Domain Preset: Supply Chain

## Description

Procurement strategy, supplier risk management, make-vs-buy analysis, logistics and transportation, inventory management, demand planning, and supply chain resilience. Emphasis on defense and aerospace supply chains with their unique regulatory, quality, and security requirements.

## Preferred Sources

1. **ISM (Institute for Supply Management) reports** — PMI indices, supply chain benchmarking, best practice guides
2. **SAM.gov** — Federal procurement data, contractor registrations, exclusion lists
3. **AS6081** — Counterfeit electronic parts avoidance standard
4. **DLA (Defense Logistics Agency)** — Government-furnished material, strategic materials, disposition services
5. **GIDEP (Government-Industry Data Exchange Program)** — Alerts, failure data, diminishing manufacturing sources
6. **Supply Chain Management Review** — Applied supply chain strategy and case studies
7. **AS9100 / AS9120** — Quality management for aerospace manufacturing and distribution
8. **DMSMS (Diminishing Manufacturing Sources and Material Shortages)** — Obsolescence management guidance
9. **Federal Procurement Data System (FPDS)** — Contract award data and spending analysis
10. **World Bank logistics reports** — Global supply chain infrastructure and risk data

## Lateral Search Strategy

| Adjacent Field | Why Cross-Pollinate |
|---------------|-------------------|
| **Epidemiology** | Network propagation models — how disruptions spread through supply networks mirrors disease transmission |
| **Ecology** | Food webs and trophic cascades model supplier dependency chains; resilience theory applies directly |
| **Game theory** | Supplier negotiation dynamics, auction design, multi-party incentive structures |
| **Network science** | Graph theory for mapping supply chain topology, identifying critical nodes and single points of failure |
| **Insurance / Actuarial science** | Risk quantification, tail-risk modeling, catastrophe modeling for supply disruptions |

## Temporal Emphasis

Current for market data and geopolitical risk assessments. Stable for methodological frameworks.

- **Half-life**: 3 years
- **Foundational corpus** (always relevant):
  - Goldratt, *The Goal* (Theory of Constraints)
  - Fisher, "What Is the Right Supply Chain for Your Product?" (HBR, 1997)
  - Lee, "The Triple-A Supply Chain" (HBR, 2004)
  - Chopra & Meindl, *Supply Chain Management* (textbook)
- **Current emphasis**: Commodity pricing, geopolitical disruption (sanctions, trade wars), lead time data, and supplier financial health change rapidly. Post-COVID supply chain restructuring and reshoring trends require current sources.

## Output Template Tweaks

Add the following sections to the standard report template:

### Supplier Risk Assessment

[Evaluate risks across dimensions: financial stability, geographic concentration, single/sole source status, quality history, capacity constraints, regulatory compliance, and geopolitical exposure. Assign risk ratings where possible.]

### Lead Time Analysis

[Document current and historical lead times for key materials or components. Identify trends, compare to industry benchmarks, and flag any items with lead times exceeding planning horizons.]

### Single/Sole Source Considerations

[Identify items with single or sole source suppliers. Assess the justification (technical, regulatory, or market-driven), the risk mitigation strategies in place, and qualification timeline for alternates if applicable.]
