# ADR-005: Council Uses Vote-First Deliberation

**Date:** 2026-03-28
**Status:** Active
**Context:** The original council design over-emphasized debate, even though the research pass suggested most value comes from ensembling independent views first. We needed a protocol that was cheaper, easier to calibrate, and still useful for governance.
**Decision:** The council runs five independent Round 1 assessments, aggregates them, and only escalates to Round 2 when consensus is weak. Debate is conditional, not mandatory.
**Consequences:** Council sessions are cheaper, easier to audit, and easier to score for calibration and value-add. The resulting vote records and parameter adjustments also create cleaner governance data for future training and review.
