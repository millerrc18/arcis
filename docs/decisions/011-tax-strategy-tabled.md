# ADR-011: Fund and Tax Structure Work Is Tabled

**Date:** 2026-03-29
**Status:** Tabled
**Context:** The fund-formation and tax research clarified the likely startup cost and timeline, but also showed that this work is premature before Arcis has a meaningful track record. The current bottleneck is proving edge, not entity structure.
**Decision:** Fund-formation and trader-tax optimization work is formally tabled until the system has a longer operating record and enough capital to justify the setup cost. It is documented, but not in the active build queue.
**Consequences:** Engineering effort stays focused on trading quality, data quality, and operational safety during Bootcamp and Micro Live. When the track record is ready, the next step is already framed rather than forgotten.
