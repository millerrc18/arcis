## What can change in v0.25.6 (mechanistic analysis)

### (a) VIX enrichment — #535

Runner at `walkforward_runner.py:354-374` persists `vix_at_entry` from `BacktestTrade.vix_at_entry`. v0.25.4 wired `lookup_vix_at_entry(entry_iso)` into `_build_trade()` (single call site covering both `_run_scheduled` and `_run_event_driven`). `lazy_prices_v1` uses `entry.kind: event_driven` (spec line 23) → hits the event-driven path.

**Mechanism says:** every OOS trade should have `vix_at_entry` populated from yfinance `^VIX` cache. No network calls at run time (cache is pre-populated).

### (b) Sections_json coverage — v0.25.5

The strategy's signal is cosine similarity of `item_1a` and `item_7` vs prior-year same-form filing. The cosine scorer reads `sections_json` on both current and prior-year rows. Two failure modes under low coverage:

1. Current filing has `NULL` sections → skip (no signal)
2. Prior-year reference has `NULL` or `{}` → can't compute similarity

Both are handled silently upstream (no signal fires). With 71.1% useful coverage (up from 28%), more current-year filings carry extractable item_1a/item_7, AND more of their prior-year references do too. **Net effect on trade count is approximately the square of the per-row coverage** for strategies requiring both current + prior-year sections. In round numbers: 0.28² = 7.8% of filing-pairs had extractable sections before; 0.71² = 50% do now. **Trade count per window could ~6× relative to v0.25.3**, though the actual signal threshold (`< 0.75` cosine) still gates which trigger.

From `walkforward_power.py:190-195`:
